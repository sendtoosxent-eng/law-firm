About KAA

A Rich Pool of Legal Professionals

KAA is a full service law firm advising clients on a wide range of legal issues across Uganda and the East African region. Our combined resources allow the firm to offer seamless legal support.

With a total of eleven partners with varied areas of specialization, along with a team of advocates, legal assistants and a full time consultant, KAA is one of the largest legal practices in Uganda.

"We strive to achieve success for our clients, investing in our people's development while attaining the highest level of competence."
